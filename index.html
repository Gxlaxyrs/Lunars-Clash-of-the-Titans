🔥 PvM Challenges

The challenges are divided into Easy, Medium, and Hard categories to accommodate all players. Each challenge must be completed as described and verified with the event keyword.

Easy Challenges 🛠️
  • King Black Dragon – Kill KBD 5 times.
  • Giant Mole – Kill Giant Mole 3 times.
  • Chaos Elemental – Kill Chaos Elemental twice.
  • Barrows – Complete 5 Barrows runs (with at least 1 unique reward).
  • Dagannoth Kings – Kill each of the three Kings (Prime, Rex, and Supreme) once.
  • God Wars Dungeon 1 – Kill any of the following bosses 3 times:
    • General Graardor
    • Commander Zilyana
    • Kree’arra
    • K’ril Tsutsaroth
  • Fight Caves – Complete a Fight Cave run.
  • Helwyr – Kill Helwyr 3 times.
  • Giant Mimic – Kill the Giant Mimic once.
  • Corrupted Creatures – Kill 10 Corrupted Creatures in the Sophanem Slayer Dungeon.
  • Queen Black Dragon (QBD) – Defeat QBD in less than 5 minutes.
  • Kalphite Queen – Kill Kalphite Queen twice.
  • Slayer Bosses – Kill any Slayer Boss (e.g., Ripper Demon or Camel Warrior) 5 times.
  • Zamorak Minions – Kill 10 minions in the Zamorakian Undercity.
  • Crystal Tree Blooms – Harvest 3 Crystal Tree Blooms while on an assigned Slayer task.
  • Reaper Assignments – Complete 1 Reaper task.
  • Dragonkin Laboratory – Kill the first boss in ED2.
  • God Wars 1 Minions – Kill 50 minions from any GWD1 faction.
  • Airut Slayer Task – Complete 15 Airut kills while on task.
  • Soul Reaper Streak – Achieve a streak of 3 completed reaper assignments.
Medium Challenges 🎖️
  • Araxxor – Kill Araxxor twice.
  • Gregorovic – Kill Gregorovic 3 times.
  • Vindicta – Kill Vindicta 3 times.
  • Kalphite King – Kill Kalphite King twice.
  • Queen Black Dragon – Complete 3 QBD kills.
  • Twin Furies – Kill the Twin Furies 3 times.
  • Nex (GWD1) – Kill Nex twice.
  • Elite Dungeons – Complete any Elite Dungeon (1, 2, or 3) once.
  • Rise of the Six – Complete a RotS run with proof of your kill count.
  • Gorvek & Vindicta – Kill Gorvek/Vindicta with a time under 2 minutes.
  • Wildy Bosses – Kill 5 different Wilderness bosses.
  • Crystal Shapeshifters – Kill 10 Crystal Shapeshifters in the Heart of Gielinor.
  • Kerapac (Normal) – Kill Kerapac twice in Normal Mode.
  • Abyssal Lords – Kill 15 Abyssal Lords.
  • Seiryu – Kill Seiryu once during ED3.
  • ED1 Boss Completion – Defeat all bosses in Elite Dungeon 1.
  • Duo Helwyr – Kill Helwyr in a duo team.
  • Warbands Clearing – Complete 2 Warbands events.
  • Raksha (Normal) – Kill Raksha twice.
Hard Challenges 🔪
  • Telos – Defeat Telos at any enrage twice.
  • Solak – Kill Solak once (solo or team).
  • Zamorakian Undercity – Complete the Zamorakian Undercity once.
  • Raksha – Kill Raksha twice.
  • Vorago – Complete a Vorago kill in any rotation.
  • The Arch-Glacor – Kill the Arch-Glacor at Hard Mode with all mechanics active.
  • Hard Mode Kerapac – Kill Kerapac in Hard Mode once.
  • Nex: Angel of Death – Kill Nex: AoD twice (solo or group).
  • 100% Enrage Boss Kill – Defeat any boss at 100% enrage or higher.
  • Master Combatant – Defeat three hard bosses (Telos, Solak, Vorago) within the event timeline.
  • Duo Solak – Kill Solak with only 2 players.
  • High Enrage Streak – Achieve a 5-kill streak at Telos with at least 200%+ enrage.
  • Team Zamorak – Complete the Zamorak boss fight with a team of 4 or less players.
  • Perfect Raksha – Kill Raksha without taking any poison damage.
  • Hard Mode Vorago – Complete Hard Mode Vorago in any rotation.
  • No Deaths – Complete ED3 with no deaths in your team.
  • Ragefire Gland Farming – Obtain 3 Ragefire Glands from high-level mobs in Kerapac’s Lair.
  • AoD Group Perfection – Achieve a kill where no one in your team dies.
  • Solo Zamorak – Defeat Zamorak in Normal Mode solo.
  • Dual Boss Challenge – Kill any two bosses within 15 minutes (e.g., Raksha and Vindicta).
